CLIENT, 20 SEP 2026: "in the footer where we have the terms, have it as Terms and Conditions and the Privacy — those two look small and do not look clickable. Make sure people can see the Terms and Conditions as well as the Privacy Policy." Three things were wrong and only one of them was the size. They were abbreviated to one word each, so "Terms" could as easily have been a section heading as a link; they were set at the weight of the copyright beside them, so nothing separated a thing you press from a thing you read; and a thin underline is not something anybody reads as a control at 13px on a dark green ground. Now they are what they are called in full, drawn as bordered pills with a document icon apiece, at the size of the rest of the footer's links. A pill with an edge round it is the one shape on a page that nobody has to be told is pressable.
