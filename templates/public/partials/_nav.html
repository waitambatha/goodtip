{% comment %} Home is deliberately absent when you are already on it — a nav item that points at the page you are reading is a dead control, and the wordmark to its left already goes there. Everywhere else it stays, because that is where it is actually useful. The two auth actions sit in their own group with a divider. They are the only items that change your state rather than move you around, so grouping them by what they DO is more honest than leaving them as the last two of nine links. Each item carries a glyph. Four of these labels are near-neighbours at a glance — News, The Wall, The Good List, Charities are all "somewhere with a list of stuff on it" until you have read them — and the glyph is what tells them apart before you finish. The stylesheet drops the glyphs at the widths where the row has no room for them, so this markup is the full design and the narrow layouts are unchanged. {% endcomment %}
