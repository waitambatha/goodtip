Both letter presentations sign off with the brand, and both used to do it with a red wax seal reading "GOOD TIPS" — a name that is not the product's, on a device that belongs to a different century. The client asked for "the right logo and the right name Good Tip". So this is the same markup as the wordmark in _nav.html and the footer, not a picture of it and not a second drawing of something close: GOOD, TIP in lime, and the full stop. One definition, three places it appears, and it cannot drift out of sync with the bar the way an image can. Colour is left to the caller — .ttb-postit and .ttb-card each set it — because the mark sits on a yellow sticky in one and a white card in the other, and white-on-white is not a mark. {% endcomment %} GOODTIP.
