A is the thing you leave on a monitor; this is the thing you paste into an email or a Slack DM, so it is drawn as a message rather than as stationery: a white card, a lime header bar carrying the mark, the note, and one quiet line of provenance under it. IT REPLACED A LINED CARD ON A DARK GREEN ENVELOPE, tilted on a desk with a pen and an inkwell beside it and a wax seal in the corner. Every one of those props was arguing that this is a letter, which is the look the client asked us to move off — and the seal was the thing actually reading "GOOD TIPS", a name the company does not have. The card is dead straight. Presentation A carries the tilt and the texture; doing it twice makes the pair look like one illustration repeated rather than two genuinely different ways to send the same words. {% endcomment %}
