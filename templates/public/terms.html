01 What GoodTip is

GoodTip is a footy tipping competition for workplaces, clubs, schools and groups of friends. Your organisation runs a comp, its members pick winners each round, and a ladder keeps score. Your organisation pays one platform fee per season and GoodTip gives a share of that fee to a charity your organisation chooses.

We are not affiliated with the AFL or the NRL, or with any club or competition. Team names and fixtures are used to describe real matches and nothing more.

02 Accounts

You need an account to tip, and you must be old enough to hold one under the law where you live. Keep your sign-in details to yourself — anything done from your account is treated as done by you. Tell us if you think somebody else has got into it.

Use a real name or a display name people in your organisation will recognise. Accounts set up to impersonate somebody else are closed.

03 No stake, no prize pool

GoodTip is free to play. There is no entry fee, no stake, no prize pool and no cash prize. What is on the line is the ladder.

You agree not to run a side pot, a buy-in, a sweep or any betting arrangement through GoodTip or on the back of a GoodTip comp. Organisations found doing it have their comp closed. This is not a technicality we are being careful about — it is the difference between what this is and what it is not.

Nothing on GoodTip is betting advice, and nothing here is an invitation to bet anywhere else.

04 The platform fee

One fee per organisation per season, priced by how many people are in the comp, in Australian dollars. It is charged when the comp launches — setting an organisation up, inviting people and running the charity vote are all free before that.

Founding Member pricing, where an organisation holds it, locks the rate charged at renewal for the seasons stated on that organisation's plan screen. It is a ceiling on what you are charged, never a floor: move up to a larger plan and you pay that plan's price, and you are never charged above the published price for the plan you are on.

Nobody playing is ever asked for money, and GoodTip never collects payment details from a participant.

05 The donation

GoodTip gives a share of the platform fee to the charity your organisation picked, from its own revenue. Your members' tips do not generate the donation and do not change its size — the fee does.

Your organisation chooses its charity from the list we make available, by vote or by an admin's decision, and can change it between seasons. Where a chosen charity cannot accept a donation, we will tell you and ask you to choose again.

Figures shown as examples on our marketing pages are labelled as examples and are not money raised.

06 If you run an organisation

Setting up an organisation makes you its admin. You can see your members' email addresses, invite and remove people, and act on their behalf inside the comp. You agree to use that only to run this comp.

Invite people who are expecting it. A list exported from somewhere else is not a reason to believe somebody wants a tipping invitation, and bulk invitations to people with no connection to your organisation are treated as spam.

You are responsible for what your organisation's admins do with it, including anyone you make an admin.

As the Admin, Manager or Captain of your organisation you are responsible for the culture and behaviour of your group. That means setting the tone, moderating the Wall and group chat, and acting on anything that falls below the standard your members expect. GoodTip can act over the top of you where something is serious enough to warrant it, but the day-to-day is yours.

07 Behaving on the Wall

The Wall and group chat are for the comp. Abuse, harassment, discrimination, threats, and content that is unlawful or obviously not welcome at somebody's work will be removed, and the account behind it can be suspended.

Your organisation's admins moderate their own comp and we can act over the top of them where something is serious enough to warrant it.

08 Fixtures, results and scoring

Fixtures and results come from public sources and are reproduced as faithfully as we can manage. They can be late, and occasionally they can be wrong. Where a result is corrected, the affected round is re-scored.

Tips lock at the start of each match. If you have not tipped a match by then, the system records the away side for you, marked as an automatic pick so it can be told apart from one you made — it counts toward your points and not toward your accuracy.

The ladder is the product of those rules applied consistently. We will fix a scoring error when we find one; we will not adjust a ladder because a result went against somebody.

09 Ending it

You can leave an organisation, or ask us to delete your account, at any time. Deleting an account removes your personal details; tips already scored on a shared ladder stay as an anonymised record, because removing them would rewrite everyone else's season.

An organisation can stop at the end of a season by not renewing. Fees already paid for a season in progress are not refunded, except where the law says otherwise or where we have not delivered what was paid for.

We can suspend or close an account or an organisation that breaks these terms, and will say why.

10 What we don't promise

GoodTip is provided as it is. We work to keep it running and accurate, but we do not promise it will be uninterrupted, that every fixture will be current, or that it will suit a purpose you have in mind for it.

Nothing here excludes any right you have under the Australian Consumer Law or other law that cannot be excluded. Where we can limit our liability, it is limited to the platform fee your organisation paid for the season in question.

11 Changes

We will update these terms as the product changes. Where a change materially affects what your organisation agreed to, we will tell the admins by email before it takes effect, and the version you agreed to at sign-up is recorded against your organisation.

These terms are governed by the law of Victoria, Australia.
