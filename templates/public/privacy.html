The short version

Your details, in one paragraph.

We hold what is needed to run the tipping comp you joined and nothing more. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. Your email address is visible to the admins of the organisations you belong to, and to nobody else playing.

01 What we collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address and password. If you join an organisation we record which organisation and group you belong to, and your role in it. As you play we store the tips you make, your results, and anything you post to a wall.

Some details are optional and only stored if you provide them — a profile photo, and a work email address where your organisation verifies membership that way.

02 Why we collect it

We use your information to run the competition you have joined: to show ladders and leaderboards, to send the emails you have asked for, to let your organisation's admins manage their members, and to record which charity your organisation is raising for. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

03 Who can see what

Other members of your organisation can see your display name, your tips once a round has locked, your position on the ladder, and anything you choose to post. Your email address is visible to the admins of the organisations you belong to. Charity votes are blind: while a vote is open nobody — including admins — can see who voted for what, and only the totals are published once it closes.

Messages you raise with your organisation's admins are private to them. Other members cannot see them, and neither can the admins of any other organisation.

The Good List shows fundraising totals by organisation only where that organisation has consented to appear, and aggregate figures are withheld until enough organisations sit behind them that no single one can be identified.

04 Service providers

{% comment %} "a payment provider to process donations" was wrong, and wrong in the one direction that matters: it described a product where money moves from members to charities through us. Nobody playing GoodTip is ever asked for money. An organisation pays one platform fee, GoodTip funds the donation from its own revenue, and the only card our payment provider ever sees belongs to the organisation. A privacy policy that contradicts the funding model on every other page is worse than one that says less — it is the document somebody reaches for when they want to know exactly what happens to their money, and this sentence told them the wrong thing. {% endcomment %}

We use third parties to deliver parts of the service: an email provider to send sign-in codes and notifications, a payment provider to take your organisation's platform fee, and hosting infrastructure to run the site. They receive only what they need to perform their function and are not permitted to use it for anything else. Participants are never asked to pay anything, so no participant's payment details are ever collected or held.

05 Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Sign-in verification codes are stored as hashes too, expire after ten minutes, and can only be used once. Access to administrative tools requires a second factor in addition to a password.

06 Your choices

You can view and change your details at any time from your profile, and turn notification emails off there. You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Some records — an organisation's payment and tax records in particular — may need to be retained where the law requires it.

07 Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and to remember which organisation you are currently viewing. Where analytics are enabled we use them to understand which pages are used, never to identify individuals.

08 Contact us

If you have a question about this policy, or want to make a privacy request, email us at hello@goodtip.com.au and we will respond.

09 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised, and material changes will be notified in the app.
