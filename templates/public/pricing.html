Good Tip, Mate. FAQ

Pricing, answered.

The questions we get most about plans and money.

Can we choose which sports we tip?

Yes. Run AFL only, NRL only, or both. Women's competitions aren't optional. AFLW comes with AFL, NRLW comes with NRL. The price is the same either way. Women's sport is structural, not decorative.

What counts toward our team size?

Active tippers in your comp. You can invite more than your tier allows during setup; you only move up a tier if you exceed the cap when the season locks.

When do we pay?

Subscribe whenever you're ready. Your card is saved and nothing is charged until 31 January 2027; after that the plan renews yearly. Setting up, inviting your people and running the Charity Vote are free.

Is the donation included in the price?

Yes. There is one flat fee, and GoodTip gives a share of it to the charity your organisation picked. Nobody in your organisation is ever asked to contribute.

What's Founding Partner pricing?

Join on or before {{ founding_closes|date:"j F Y" }} and the rate you sign up at is the rate you're charged for three seasons. The first of those is a stub rather than a full year: it runs from the day you join through the end of the {{ founding_stub_ends|date:"Y" }} season, however many months that happens to be. Two full seasons follow it — {{ founding_seasons|slice:"1:"|join:" and " }} — whatever list prices do in the meantime. You also get first access to new sports and features in {{ founding_stub_ends|date:"Y" }}. Move up a plan and you pay that plan's price; you're never charged above list. After the window the price rises for new joiners only — if you are already in, your rate is untouched.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Grow past your plan and you move up to the next one, at that plan's price. A Founding rate stays with the plan it was locked on, and you're never charged above the published price for the plan you're on.
