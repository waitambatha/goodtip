Good Tip, Mate. FAQ

Charities, answered.

Where the money comes from, and who decides where it goes.

Who pays for the donation?

GoodTip does, out of your organisation's one flat fee. Tipping is free for everyone playing, and nobody in your organisation is ever asked to contribute.

Who chooses the charity?

Your organisation, in the Charity Vote. Run it while your people are joining, before the season starts, and its result is the charity your season supports.

What's a hands-on activity partner?

A charity you can do something with, not just give to: warehouse shifts with Foodbank, build days with Habitat for Humanity, a clean-up with Clean Up Australia, food rescue with OzHarvest. Something your whole organisation can do together.

Can we support a charity that isn't listed?

Yes. Once you're in, an organisation admin can add your own charity from Manage → Charities, and GoodTip checks it before it goes live. If you're a charity that wants to be in the pool, get in touch below.
