AFL, AFLW, NRL and NRLW. Weekly tips, a live ladder, and the running charity total in the same place. State of Origin picks are worth four points; finals worth two. The Wall keeps the banter and the tally front and centre all season long.

Roles

Two roles. One season.

Team Manager

Sets it up. Keeps it running.

Creates the comp and sets it up for the season. Runs the Charity Vote and invites the organisation. The one whose name is on it, in the good way.

Captain

Nominated. Then owns the season.

Voted in by the organisation to keep the energy up: chases stragglers, stokes the Wall, keeps the tips coming every round. Every great comp has one.
