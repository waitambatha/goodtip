01

Set up

Set up your organisation. Run the Charity Vote.

Name your comp and invite your team. No payment until you're ready to launch. While the organisation fills, run the Charity Vote. Everyone nominates, everyone votes, majority wins. That settles where GoodTip's giving is headed before a single game is tipped.

02

Tip

Tip and track, every round.

AFL, AFLW, NRL and NRLW. Weekly tips, a live ladder, and the running charity total in the same place. State of Origin picks are worth four points; finals worth two. The Wall keeps the banter and the tally front and centre all season long.

03

Impact

Season done well.

Last grand final done. The Impact Report goes out: total raised, charity named, tipping results, the lot. One number your board can cite and your organisation can be proud of. Season done well.
