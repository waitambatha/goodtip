Why we built it

A tipping comp is already a community. It was just missing a cause.

Every season, offices, clubs, pubs and family group chats run a footy tipping comp. People check the ladder on Monday, argue about a Thursday night upset, and give the winner a trophy nobody keeps. It works because it is small, regular and social — the same three things that make fundraising hard.

GoodTip keeps all of that and changes one thing at the end: the money lands somewhere good. Your organisation picks a charity, or puts it to a vote and lets the members decide. Everything raised goes there.

Tipping stays free for everyone playing. We are not trying to change how anybody spends their Friday — only where the pot ends up.

What we stand on

Three things we will not trade away.

Everything else about GoodTip is up for debate. These are not.

Free to play

Nobody pays to enter a tipping comp on GoodTip. If a member has to reach for a card to join in, we have built the wrong thing.

01

The money is traceable

Every dollar is tied to the charity that was active when it was raised. The Good List shows what has actually been settled — never a projection dressed up as a total.

02

The group decides

Which cause you back is your organisation's call, not ours. Pick it, or hold a vote and let every member have a say.

03

Where the money comes from

Nobody in the comp puts a dollar in.

This is the part people ask about first, so it is worth being plain about.

Your organisation subscribes

One platform fee for the season, paid by the organisation when the first round locks in. Not by the people tipping.

01

GoodTip gives a share of it

A share of that fee goes to the charity your organisation is backing. It comes out of our revenue, not out of anybody's pay packet.

02

You get the receipt

At the end of the season the Impact Report goes out: total raised, charity named, tipping results. One number your board can cite.

03

The bit that lasts

The comp is over by October. The money is still doing something in March.

Straight answers

The questions we get before anyone signs up.

Do our people pay anything?

No. Tipping is free for every participant, every round, all season. The only invoice GoodTip sends goes to the organisation.

Who picks the charity?

Your organisation. Choose one outright, or run the Charity Vote — everyone nominates, everyone votes, and the ballot stays blind until it closes.

What happens to our data?

It runs the comp and nothing else. We do not sell personal information and we do not pass it to advertisers — the Privacy Policy says exactly what we hold and why.

How long does setting up take?

A few minutes. Name the comp, invite your people, run the vote while the organisation fills. You pay nothing until the first tips lock in.
