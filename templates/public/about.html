{% ptext "about" "hero.title" %}The comp you already run, doing something else as well.{% endptext %}

{% ptext "about" "hero.sub" %}GoodTip started with a simple observation: hundreds of thousands of Australians already tip the footy at work every winter, and none of it goes anywhere.{% endptext %}

{% ptext "about" "story.eyebrow" %}Why we built it{% endptext %}

{% ptext "about" "story.title" %}A tipping comp is already a community. It was just missing a cause.{% endptext %}

{% ptext "about" "story.p1" %}Every season, offices, clubs, pubs and family group chats run a footy tipping comp. People check the ladder on Monday, argue about a Thursday night upset, and give the winner a trophy nobody keeps. It works because it is small, regular and social — the same three things that make fundraising hard.{% endptext %}

{% ptext "about" "story.p2" %}GoodTip keeps all of that and changes one thing at the end: the money lands somewhere good. Your organisation picks a charity, or puts it to a vote and lets the members decide. Everything raised goes there.{% endptext %}

{% ptext "about" "story.p3" %}Tipping stays free for everyone playing. We are not trying to change how anybody spends their Friday — only where the pot ends up.{% endptext %}
