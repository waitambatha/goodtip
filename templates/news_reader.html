"Make the part that is the 2/3 visible but translucent, so someone can know we have data there — something that they see but cannot see." Real text, blurred and faded out, so the page reads as an article that carries on rather than as grey placeholder bars — which is the whole difference between "there is more here" and "this is a skeleton loader". It is a SCREENFUL, not the whole remainder. Anything sent to a browser can be read by anyone who looks, so serving the rest of the story under a blur would publish it to everybody and to every crawler while merely inconveniencing a reader. The effect does not need it: nobody can read blurred text either way and the gate sits immediately underneath.
