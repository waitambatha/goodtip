{% comment %} THE MEMBER APP'S THEME SWITCH. Same three-segment shape, same storage and same attribute as the ones in /admin/ and in the HQ shell (partials/_gt_theme_switch.html): it writes `localStorage.theme` and ``. So a choice made on any GoodTip screen is still in force on every other one, and no area has to know the others exist. WHAT "DARK" AND "LIGHT" MEAN HERE, WHICH IS NOT WHAT THEY MEAN IN /admin/. The member app's designed look is the deep green — that IS its dark theme, and it is the default. The light theme is the same screens on cream with green as an accent rather than as the ground. AUTO DOES NOT MEAN "FOLLOW THE DEVICE" ON THESE PAGES, and that asymmetry is deliberate. In HQ the light palette is the design and the dark one is the accommodation, so following prefers-color-scheme is the right default. Here it is the other way round: the green is the product. Having `auto` quietly hand the majority of visitors — anyone whose phone is on dark — a look nobody has signed off would be a redesign by side effect. So `auto` keeps the green, and light is something you choose. Three segments rather than a cycling icon because a control that changes meaning on every press cannot be aimed: you want light, you press once, you get light. {% endcomment %}
