The codes are a band across the top of the card rather than chips floating over the photograph — which is where they were, and which made them look like a watermark ON the picture rather than a label OF the story. ONE SHAPE, ONE SIZE. The bottom row used to be a second, smaller "picture beside words" card ("why is it small have all of them same size"). Both rows render this, and every part of it is fixed or clamped — the band, the picture, two lines of headline, two lines of teaser, the footer — so all six cards are the same height whichever stories they are showing, and a card that turns over cannot change size under the reader. THE WHOLE CARD IS THE LINK, and there is still only one link in it. The headline's anchor is stretched over the card by .ndc-a::after, so a press anywhere opens the story, "Read more" is a label rather than a second control aimed at the same address, and a screen reader is handed one link with the headline as its name instead of six cards all called "Read more". Expects `p` (a NewsPost). {% endcomment %}
{% comment %} THE COLOUR, PICKED BY COMPETITION — and for a story about more than one, a fusion that does not muddy them ("if its a mixture blog of different competition lets it have a slick color fusion that do not mix, find a way"). So the band is one hard-edged segment per code, sharing the width — the colours meet without ever blending — and the only blended thing is the halo behind it, which is out of focus and carries no edge for a mixed colour to appear on.
