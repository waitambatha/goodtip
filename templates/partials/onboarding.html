{% comment %} THE PER-PAGE WALKTHROUGH. The client's reason for it, in his words: "just enough to stop first-week confusion getting mistaken for bugs." That is the whole brief, and it sets the bar — each page points at a few things and gets out of the way. It is not a product tour and should never grow into one. WHAT IS HERE AND WHAT IS NOT. This file is only the mechanism: the ring, the bubble, where to put them, and telling the server when it has been put away. Which page gets which steps, and the copy, is in accounts/onboarding.py. That split is why this include sits in app_base.html and works on every private page without knowing what any of them are. WHY IT IS BUILT AROUND MISSING TARGETS. A page's steps do not all exist on every render of it — the charity banner only appears while a vote is open, the sidebar is off-screen on a phone, and a manager's page has controls a member's does not. So each step names an element by data-coach, and any step whose element is not on the page, or not visible, is dropped before the tour starts. A bubble pointing at nothing is precisely the confusion this exists to prevent. It is also entirely optional scenery: no target, no tour, and the page is unchanged. Nothing here is required for any page to work. {% endcomment %}
