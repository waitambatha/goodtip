{% comment %} The theme control for the GoodTip HQ shell, as a segmented switch. Same three-segment shape as the one in /admin/, and deliberately the same storage: it writes `localStorage.theme` and ``, which is exactly what Django's admin reads. So a choice made here is still in force after clicking through to /admin/ and back, and neither side has to know the other exists. Three segments rather than a cycling icon because a control that changes meaning on every press cannot be aimed: you want dark, you press once, you get light. Here every option is on screen and the one in use is filled. {% endcomment %}
