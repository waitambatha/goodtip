ASKED FOR: "the dropdown will show All by default; beside it let's have the last 5 rounds, with a button to keep on moving backward, and display what round it's showing — and on the round like 26 it must be highlighted so I know I am there, and be able to click the 25 and see the ranking of that round." WHY A WINDOW AND NOT A LIST. A league that has been running has eighty rounds. Five is enough to hold a month and short enough that each one is a target rather than something to read; the window walks a page at a time, which is a different gesture from the arrows either side of the dropdown and covers a different distance. The window is anchored on the newest round because that is the direction people look — this week, then last week — and a chosen round is always inside it, so a link to round 3 in August opens with round 3 on screen rather than showing the last five and leaving the reader to hunt. See tipping.views.round_strip. Expects: strip (from round_strip), base, param, keep, all_label. Each button arrives carrying what its own link should say — the leaderboard posts round IDs, because a round number names one round per competition and on an unfiltered board the number 4 is up to five different rounds.
