"On the share button, let's have the share button and the name over it, something of a good size that will be visible, and after clicking we must see the social media platforms — so when I press share my social media is open and I am able to share. If I click Facebook that should take me to my Facebook, where I will share with my friends." So it is one large, labelled button rather than a row of small unlabelled icons, and pressing it opens the platforms. Each is that platform's own share address, so pressing Facebook lands on Facebook's share box with the story already in it, signed in as whoever is at the keyboard — there is no GoodTip account in between and nothing to connect. They are ordinary links, so they work with JavaScript off; with it, gt-share.js opens the panel, sends the desktop ones to a pop-up window rather than taking the reader off the story, and adds the phone's own share sheet where there is one. Expects `prefix` (the page's class prefix — "rd" on the reader), `post` and `share_url`. {% endcomment %}
