{% comment %} Sharing a story, and its sources. IT OPENS SIDEWAYS (Sep 2026, client): "the share button when I click it you see the feel that you get on a menu collapse that it comes — on that row that will have that share, because it's already here, so when I click it it should be like it's opening from left to right and have socials that someone clicks will take them to their respective socials and share." So the platforms are not a panel that drops underneath any more. They live on the same row as the button, to its right, in a strip that is zero wide until the button is pressed and then wipes open left to right — the way a collapsed menu opens — with each platform arriving a beat after the one before it. See .shr-reveal in goodtip.css: the width is animated with grid-template-columns 0fr -> 1fr, which is the one way to transition to "as wide as the content is" without a script measuring anything. Each is that platform's own share address, so pressing Facebook lands on Facebook's share box with the story already in it, signed in as whoever is at the keyboard — there is no GoodTip account in between and nothing to connect. They are ordinary links, so they work with JavaScript off; with it, gt-share.js opens the strip, sends the desktop ones to a pop-up window rather than taking the reader off the story, and adds the phone's own share sheet where there is one. Expects `prefix` (the page's class prefix — "rd" on the reader), `post` and `share_url`. {% endcomment %}
