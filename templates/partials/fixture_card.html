WHY THIS EXISTS Each club used to be named three or four times on the same card: once in the matchup line, once as the thing you press to tip it, once in the MatchReader verdict, and once again in each form row. Four "Fremantle"s is not emphasis, it is noise, and it made the card twice as tall as the information in it. Here a club appears ONCE. The club IS the control: press it to tip it, and the card shows which one you picked. Its form rides on the same row, so the comparison you are being asked to make is a single left-to-right read.
