One card per internal leaderboard the organisation runs (AFL, AFLW, NRL, NRLW). While a competition's season is still going, its card says why there is nothing to download yet — the certificate only exists once the board is final and locked (§4). After that, every champion is listed (co-champions each get their own, identical certificate — §2) with the two exports the spec requires: the landscape PDF for print and the 1080×1080 PNG for social, plus the optional 9:16 story PNG (§5). "Share" opens the phone's own share sheet with the square image where the browser supports it — no social account to connect, which is all §5 asks for. Lives on the org side: admins, captains and team managers (tipping.views._can_certify), not the winner's own account. {% endcomment %} {% block body_class %}page-deep sheet-page{% endblock %} {% block page_backdrop %}{% include "partials/page_scenes.html" %}{% endblock %} {% block app_content %}
