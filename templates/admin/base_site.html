Four items in a flat list, each wearing a hairline that Django's own base.css draws under every `#user-tools a` — so the menu arrived with a line ruled across each row, uppercased and set at 11px, which is what the client saw. The overrides for that live in gtadmin.css under "Out-specifying admin/base.css"; they have to be there rather than here because Django styles those elements by id and an id beats any class selector however it is written. What is here is the arrangement. A card at the top saying WHO is signed in — name, the whole address, and the level of access — because the bar itself only has room for a short name, and "which account am I in?" is the question this menu is opened to answer as often as any of its links. Then the items in two labelled groups: the two that change your own account, and the one that leaves the admin. Signing out is last, alone, and the only red thing in the menu. {% endcomment %}
