A GALLERY, NOT A TABLE. The thing somebody is looking for here is a picture, and they will recognise it in a tenth of the time it takes them to read a filename. So the tile is the picture, big enough to be recognised, and the name is underneath it in small type for when two shots of the same ground need telling apart. THE FILTERS ARE PLAIN LINKS, not a form. Every state of this screen is an address — somebody can send "the videos" to a colleague, and the back button walks back through what they looked at. A GET form would do the same thing with more machinery and a worse URL.
