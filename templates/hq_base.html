The three pages that moved out of the organisation admin when the two areas were split — the sync panel, the public site's enquiries, and the news editor. None of them belongs to any organisation: an enquiry is addressed to GoodTip the company, the news feed is the company's, and sync drives the platform's fixtures for everybody. They live under /admin/ URLs now, but keep this shell rather than Django's admin chrome. Their markup is built from goodtip.css components — the sync panel alone is 13KB of them — and rebuilding all of it in the Django admin's component set to change a menu would have been the expensive way round. Same design language as the org admin and as /admin/: cream, white panels, a rail that collapses. {% endcomment %} {% include "public/partials/_icons.html" %}
