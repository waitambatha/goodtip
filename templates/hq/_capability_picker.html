That pairing is the whole feature — "write the blog, but I see it first" — so it is one row rather than two separate lists you have to keep mentally aligned. Grouped into one
per area. Open by default, because the point of the screen is that somebody reads the nineteen things before consenting to them, and a stack of shut drawers invites ticking the headings instead. The checkboxes are visually hidden and drawn as .ad-box beside them rather than restyled: a native box cannot be given a 6px radius and a brand green consistently across browsers, and this screen is judged on whether nineteen of them look like one set. `held_keys` / `reviewed_keys` pre-tick it when editing somebody who already exists; both are empty when adding. {% endcomment %}
