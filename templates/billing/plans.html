"The five pricing cards must each have a completely different, tasteful background colour ... do not make any of them green, lime, pure white, cream or beige." Five plans in five shades of the same green was five identical cards with different numbers on them, which is the one thing a pricing page must not be: the whole job is to make five options feel like five choices. So each card takes a soft pastel of its own — sky, lavender, peach, mist, rose — and the brand keeps every mark ON them: the tick, the price, the border, the badge, the button. `data-tier` carries it rather than a class per plan, so a sixth plan added in the admin gets a colour from the same list instead of rendering grey until somebody remembers this file. The palette is in goodtip.css. {% endcomment %}
