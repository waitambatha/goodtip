Why this exists as its own screen rather than a section of the organisation's: groups stopped being departments. A franchise — the client's example was McDonald's, several restaurants in one city — is one organisation whose groups are separate businesses with their own staff and their own local causes. The Sydney store backing Ronald McDonald House while head office backs Beyond Blue is the feature, not a conflict. The ballot is the organisation's charity list. A group does not invent a charity; the org adds it once in Manage → Charities and every group can then vote for it. Blind vote, exactly as the org-wide one: counts while open, tallies only once closed. That rule is implemented once, in views._ballot_context. {% endcomment %}
