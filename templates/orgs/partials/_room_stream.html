{% comment %} THE SCROLLING HALF OF A CONVERSATION — and the only part the poll replaces. WHY THIS IS ITS OWN FILE, and it is not a tidiness argument. The first version polled the whole conversation pane, composer included. That works right up until somebody is halfway through typing when the twelve seconds are up: the swap replaces the textarea they are typing into with a fresh empty one, and their message is gone with no way to get it back. Same for a chosen attachment, a reply they had picked, and a voice note recorded but not yet sent. So the poll's target is exactly this: the messages, and nothing that holds state the reader has created. The header, the composer and the recorder sit outside it and are never touched. It carries its own hx-* attributes because hx-swap="outerHTML" replaces this element with the response — so the response has to BE this element, poll and all, or the conversation updates once and then stops. Expects: entries, thread, thread_org, can_pin. {% endcomment %}
