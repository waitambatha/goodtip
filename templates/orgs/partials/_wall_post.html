{% load humanize cache %}{% comment %} ONE POST ON THE WALL, AS A MESSAGE (client, 5 Nov 2026): "let it be a message in itself, then I have the option to reply to that specific message ... do not attach it, let it be a button." So a post is a chat bubble with nothing bolted under it. Replying is a button under the bubble, a swipe across it, or a press (a hold on touch) that opens its menu — all three fill the ONE composer at the foot of the room with a quote of the post, and the reply goes into this post's thread. The thread folds away under "N replies" so the room reads as posts, not as a column of reply boxes. Recaps and GoodTip updates are not anybody's message, so they stay full-width cards in the middle of the room, with the same Reply button. The row is [data-msg] so gt-chat.js treats it like a message: swipe, press menu, edit-in-place. Its id is "p" so the composer can tell a reply to the post from a reply to one of its replies (plain ids) — see wall.html. Expects `p` (a WallPost from orgs.views._wall_posts_context), plus the page's `org`, `group`, `user` and `room`. {% endcomment %}
