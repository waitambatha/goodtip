So the member list, the pinned message and a direct message's profile card all moved into that panel (orgs/partials/_room_- details.html), and the width they were using went to the conversation. WHY THE SUPPORT THREADS ARE STILL IN THE LIST. They are the same thing as everything else in it: an exchange between a member and the people who run their comp. They keep their own row style and their own status chips, the admin's end of them (/manage/messages/) is untouched, and the member's private line to the admins still exists. What went is the idea that it was the whole screen. The client's own reasoning holds: every member list now tags who the admins are, so a direct message is the obvious way to reach one. ONE PAGE, TWO VIEWS ON A PHONE. There is no separate mobile template. The same markup is a two-column desktop layout and, on a phone, a WhatsApp-style stack — list, then conversation — where `data-view` on the wrapper says which level you are on and the back arrow walks out of it. The details panel is the same panel at every width; on a phone it simply covers the whole conversation.
