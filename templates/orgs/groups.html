Does {{ org.name }} need groups?

A group is a team inside your organisation — Marketing, IT, Sales, the Melbourne office. Everyone still tips the same fixtures under the same rules for the same charity. What a group adds is its own ladder and its own wall, so Marketing can beat IT without either of them leaving the comp.

Worth it when

There are enough people that one ladder stops being personal — a few hundred, spread across teams who already talk about each other.

Not worth it when

There are five or ten of you. One ladder is the whole point, and splitting it just gives everyone a smaller thing to win.

You can switch this on later — in a year, or in three — and nothing is lost by waiting. Switching it off again keeps every group and every tip exactly where it is.
