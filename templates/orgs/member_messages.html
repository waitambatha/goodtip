This page used to be scoped to a single organisation: whichever one the nav happened to be pointing at. That is where the client hit it. They raised something with the admins of one of their organisations, pressed Messages, and were told "Nothing yet" — by the page for a different organisation. Nothing on the screen said which organisation it was showing, and there was no way to get to the others. Mail does not work like that. You have one inbox, and each item says who it is from. So the list is now every thread this member can read across every membership, each row naming its organisation, and the composer asks which organisation you are writing to instead of assuming. The composer is still a sheet rather than a form sitting permanently on the page. Writing to your admins is something you do occasionally; the thing you come back for is the answer. {% endcomment %}
