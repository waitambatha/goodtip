Running automatically

On the server's own clock, whether or not anybody is on the site. A visitor arriving at 3am finds last night's results already in.

Fixturesevery hour New games and changed kickoffs, for every competition below.
Live scoresevery 2 minutes Score, period and clock while a game is being played — and the moment a game turns final, its result and ladder are pulled in on the spot, not on the next tick.
Resultsevery 15 minutes Final scores and backstop for anything the live poll's own trigger missed. This is what grades tips and awards points.
Laddersevery 30 minutes Computed from our own results — no outside source to fail.
Full-season sweepevery 6 hours Re-reads every round of every competition, so a game added mid-season or a round missed while a feed was down is picked up without anyone asking.
Feed checkevery hour, and after every sweep Compares every game on the AFL and NRL sites with what every league shows, re-syncs anything that differs, and emails the team about anything it could not fix.

How it works

  1. Choose your org & round

    Select the organisation, competition and round you want to update.

  2. Choose what to sync

    Fixtures brings in upcoming games. Results brings in completed scores.

  3. We do the rest

    We pull the data and place it right into your org.

Safe & secure

Nothing is overwritten outside the round you pick, and results only ever grade tips already submitted.
