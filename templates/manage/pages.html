How editing a page works

{{ total_edits }} edit{{ total_edits|pluralize }} saved across the site.
  • Everything on the page is editable. Every heading, paragraph, caption, button label and picture. Click the words to type over them, click a picture to swap it.
  • Nothing is live until you press Save — except pictures, which save as soon as they finish uploading.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes, the same as anywhere else. So does the undo button in the toolbar.
  • An edit is tied to the wording it replaced. If that original wording is later changed in the site itself, the edit stops applying and is listed above rather than being silently pasted over something it was never written for.
  • Shared furniture is edited per page. The top menu and the footer appear on every page, but an edit made here belongs to the page it was made on — change the menu on Home and Home is what changes.
  • Numbers and names that the page works out for itself — a member's name, a ladder position, a total raised — can be typed over but will not stay changed. They are recalculated on every visit.
