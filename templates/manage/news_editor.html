{% comment %} WHEN IT GOES OUT — a button, not a form field (Sep 2026, client: "I do not like how that automated publishing where it will auto post. Let it be a button, then when I click, a nice time and calendar UI comes in"). The old scheduler was a date box that was always open and always filled in with "now", so every story looked like it was being scheduled and a stray keystroke in it could hold a story back by a month. Now the default is said in words — it goes out when you save — and scheduling is a thing you ASK for: the button opens a calendar and a clock, and until you pick something the hidden field is empty and the view uses the moment of saving. See the "WHEN IT GOES OUT" script in gt-news-editor.js. Still one field underneath (`published_at`), and still no cron: every reader-facing query asks for `published_at <= now`, so a scheduled story simply arrives. The calendar can also go backwards, which is how a story written up from last weekend is filed under last weekend. {% endcomment %}
