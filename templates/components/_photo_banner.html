Built as a component because the same shape kept being wanted in five places and each would otherwise have grown its own slightly different version. Takes: image path under static/, e.g. "img/scenes/nrl-players-fans.jpg" eyebrow small label above the heading (optional) heading the line that carries the moment lead a sentence under it (optional) tall "1" for a taller banner where the photo is the point align "center" to centre the text; default is left Two rules the photos forced: * A SCRIM, always. These are real match-day phone shots, not art-directed plates: the sky in one is bright dusk, another is a lit scoreboard at night. White text over the raw image is legible on some and not on others, and "some" is not a standard. The gradient runs dark at the bottom where the text sits and lifts toward the top so the photo still reads as a photo. * object-position on the focal point rather than a bare cover. A crowd shot cropped to its centre is a photo of somebody's shoulder; these are framed low, so the subject survives the crop at banner ratios. {% endcomment %}
