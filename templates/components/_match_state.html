{% comment %} Match state, betting-board style. Expects `match`. Three shapes, one per phase: live — a pulsing dot, the clock ("Q3 12:45") and time since kick-off, in the card's top line beside the tip state. The score itself lives in each team's box (client, 31 Oct 2026), so the poll sends those two numbers back out-of-band (`oob`, tipping.views). complete — final score plus when it finished upcoming — kickoff day/time and how long until it starts Venue and city come from match.venue_label so the ground shows everywhere the fixture does. REFRESHING ITSELF The server pulls in-play scores every two minutes, and until now none of that reached a page somebody already had open: the score changed in the database and the reader watched a stale number until they thought to reload. A live badge that never moves is worse than no live badge. So a match in play re-fetches just this block. The poll is on the block rather than the page because this is the only part that changes mid-game, and it stops on its own — once the fixture is complete the response comes back without a trigger, so a finished game costs nothing for the rest of the day. Thirty seconds against a two-minute feed cadence: fast enough that a score is never more than one poll behind the sync, slow enough to stay cheap. {% endcomment %}
