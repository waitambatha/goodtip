{% comment %} One charity as a TILE, for grids where the charities are the content. The sibling _charity_card.html is a row — a logo, a name and a line of text — built for ballots and summaries where the charity sits inside somebody else's sentence. This is the other shape: the logo carried at a size you can actually recognise, because on the charity screens the question is "which of these do I know?" and a 44px mark does not answer it. The initials tile is a designed state, not a broken image. Plenty of charities publish no usable icon — bot-protected sites, a 16px favicon of mush — and its colour is derived from the name, so the same charity is the same colour here as on the ballot. A tile becomes a LINK when `edit_url` is given and the charity is one this viewer may change (charity.can_edit, set by the view). That is how a logo the automatic fetch could not find gets supplied: press the charity, land on the form. Without an edit_url it stays a plain tile, which is what the ballot and the summaries want — those are places you read a charity, not places you change one.
