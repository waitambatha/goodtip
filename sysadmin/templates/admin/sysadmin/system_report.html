{# ---- headline numbers ---- #} {% comment %} EIGHT TILES, IN TWO READINGS. The first four are the platform: people, organisations, memberships, tips. The second four are the things an admin actually opens during a week — news, pages, enquiries, the team — and none of them had a number anywhere, so "is there anything in News?" or "how many people can get into HQ?" meant opening the page to find out. Tips became a link — it was a plain div on a screen whose whole job is to be the way in. Memberships stays a tile because OrgMember is not registered in the admin, so there is no list to send anybody to. The glyphs are chosen to be told apart at a glance rather than to be literal: Members and Memberships are the same noun twice, so one is a person and the other is a crowd; News is a single document and Pages is a stack of them, because the difference between them is that one is a story and the other is the whole site. {% endcomment %}
